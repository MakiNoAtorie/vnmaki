Assets of this project
=====================

images/, audio/, models/ and shaders/ were made by the script tools/generators/template_assets.py
of VNMaki (https://github.com/MakiNoAtorie/vnmaki), from rectangles, ellipses and sine waves. No
other artwork, music or voice file was used.

movies/clip.webm is the tiny test clip of VNMaki (tests/fixtures/video/): six solid-color frames
and a 440 Hz tone, AV1 and Opus in WebM. The ffmpeg command that made it is in
tests/fixtures/video/README.md of the repository.

game, with no credit. Replace them with your own. The two fonts are not CC0: they are under the

The Reserved Font Name of OpenDyslexic means that a changed copy of the font must not be called
OpenDyslexic. A font subset is a changed copy, so project.toml lists the font under
[build.fonts] keep: `vnmaki build windows` and `vnmaki import` copy it unchanged, and do not make a
subset for each language. Do the same for every font of your game that has a Reserved Font Name,
and keep the license text of each font in your game. Noto Sans may be subset.

The engine loads fonts/dyslexia.otf (or fonts/dyslexia.ttf) and puts it first in the font chain
while the player has "Dyslexia-friendly font" on. Noto Sans covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic with
accents. For a game in another language, add a font for it under [fonts.fallback].

This game has one language, English. `vnmaki new <name> --lang ja` adds Japanese: the add-on
templates/addons/ja/ puts its own assets file, ASSETS.ja.txt, next to this one, with the font
Noto Sans JP, its license text and translations/ja.po.

plugins/flash/ is a Lua plugin (the transition "flash") that was written for VNMaki. It is CC0 1.0 like the rest.
