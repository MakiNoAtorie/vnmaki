Assets of this project
=====================

images/, audio/, shaders/ and fonts/default.ttf were made by the script
tools/generators/template_assets.py of VNMaki (https://github.com/MakiNoAtorie/vnmaki), from
rectangles, ellipses and sine waves. No other artwork, music, voice or font file was used.

movies/clip.webm is the tiny test clip of VNMaki (tests/fixtures/video/): six solid-color frames
and a 440 Hz tone, AV1 and Opus in WebM. The ffmpeg command that made it is in
tests/fixtures/video/README.md of the repository.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
You may use, change and sell them with your game, with no credit. Replace them with your own.

  images/bg/classroom.png            backdrop
  images/bg/rooftop.png              backdrop of the presentation demo
  images/eileen/*.png                layers of the sprite "eileen": base, outfit, face, eyelids
                                     (the frames of the blink) and mouth (the frames of lip sync)
  images/mask/iris.png               mask of a transition
  audio/bgm/theme.wav                music loop
  audio/voice/eileen/hello.wav       a voice line of tone bursts, for lip sync
  shaders/sepia.frag.hlsl            a game shader
  movies/clip.webm                   a short movie
  fonts/default.ttf                  5 by 7 pixel font, ASCII only
  fonts/dyslexia.ttf                 PLACEHOLDER for the dyslexia-friendly font (see below)
  styles/high_contrast.lua           the look of prefs.high_contrast, written for VNMaki

fonts/dyslexia.ttf is the same pixel font as fonts/default.ttf with wider letter spacing. It is a
placeholder, not a real dyslexia-friendly font: no such font with a suitable license is in this
repository. Before you ship a game, replace it with a real font under the SIL Open Font License,
for example OpenDyslexic (https://opendyslexic.org/), under the same name, and record its license
here. The engine loads fonts/dyslexia.ttf when it exists and puts it first in the font chain while
the player has "Dyslexia-friendly font" on.

translations/ja.po has one translated line (the narration of the label warm) to show the Japanese language, which is written in columns. With this font the line shows as boxes.

The font has no accents or non-Latin letters. For a game in another language, use a font that
has them, and list it under [fonts.fallback] in project.toml.
