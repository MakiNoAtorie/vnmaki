Assets of this project
=====================

images/, audio/ and fonts/default.ttf were made by the script
tools/generators/template_assets.py of VNMaki (https://github.com/MakiNoAtorie/vnmaki), from
rectangles, ellipses and sine waves. No other artwork, music or font file was used.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
You may use, change and sell them with your game, with no credit. Replace them with your own.

  images/bg/classroom.png            backdrop
  images/eileen/*.png                layers of the sprite "eileen"
  audio/bgm/theme.wav                music loop
  fonts/default.ttf                  5 by 7 pixel font, ASCII only

The font has no accents or non-Latin letters. For a game in another language, use a font that
has them, and list it under [fonts.fallback] in project.toml.
