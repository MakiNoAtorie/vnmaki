Japanese add-on
===============

These files came with `vnmaki new <name> --lang ja` (the add-on templates/addons/ja/ of VNMaki,
https://github.com/MakiNoAtorie/vnmaki).

  fonts/NotoSansJP.ttf    Noto Sans JP, a variable font, Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe.
                          https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp
                          SIL Open Font License 1.1: licenses/NotoSansJP-OFL.txt. It is the
                          fallback of "ja" in project.toml ([fonts.fallback]). Its Reserved Font
                          Name is "Source", which the name "Noto Sans JP" does not use, so a subset
                          of this font may keep its name. Keep the license text in your game.
  translations/ja.po      The output of `vnmaki extract-strings`. It translates the narration of
                          the label warm, the menu option that leads to the content demo and every
                          line of the label content, to show the Japanese language, which is written
                          in columns. CC0 1.0, like the rest of the template.

project.toml has `"ja"` in `languages`, and `[vertical] ja = true` and `[fonts.fallback] ja`.
